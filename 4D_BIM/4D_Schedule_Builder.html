4D Schedule Builder

Load a Primavera P6 XER, pick the building, and enter the scope you read from the drawings (work packages, zones, quantities). Every P6 activity becomes a group whose baseline, actual and current dates stay identical to P6; the detailed activities are sequenced inside each group window. Output: the 4D Excel workbook (schedule, BIM element map, TimeLiner import, Gantt, P6 roll-up) and a TimeLiner CSV.
