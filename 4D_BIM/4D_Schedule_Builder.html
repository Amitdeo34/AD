4D Schedule Builder

Upload the drawings (a ZIP with any folder structure) and the group-level schedule (P6 XER or Excel). The tool reads every drawing, splits them by area and checks them against the schedule. For the area you pick, it reads the drawings in detail and builds the 4D schedule: every schedule activity becomes a group whose baseline, actual and current dates stay identical to the schedule, with detailed activities and approximate quantities sequenced inside. Output: the 4D Excel workbook and a TimeLiner CSV.

1

Inputs

Files are read in your browser. Only the drawing images you ask Claude to read leave it.

Group schedule

P6 XER, or an Excel export with Activity ID, Name, BL Start / Finish, Start, Finish (and Actual Start / Finish, % complete).

Drawings

Pick the drawings folder on your computer (all sub-folders are included, no ZIP needed), or add a ZIP / PDF / DXF / image files. The files are read here in your browser, not uploaded to a server. DWG cannot be read in a browser – keep the PDF of those drawings in the folder.
