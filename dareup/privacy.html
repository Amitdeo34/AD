DareUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: [effective date]

In short
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Your game data (player names, scores, settings and your own packs) stays on your device.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see your card or UPI details.
  • Our server stores nothing about you. It only checks a payment with Razorpay and hands back a premium pass.

Who we are

DareUp is a Truth or Dare party game made by [Developer name] ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [contact email].

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser or app storage on your own device. It is not sent to us.

  • Player names or nicknames you type in, scores and the game in progress
  • Your settings (theme, sound, vibration, timer and skip limit)
  • Truths and dares you write in your own packs
  • Which cards have come up, so they do not repeat
  • If you buy Premium: your premium pass (a signed code holding the plan, the Razorpay payment ID and order ID, and when the pass starts and ends) and the premium packs downloaded for offline play

You can delete all of it at any time with Settings → Delete all data on this device, by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

Payments

When you buy a premium pass, the payment is processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. You enter your payment details (such as UPI, card, net banking or wallet details, email and phone number) into Razorpay's checkout, not into DareUp. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to that information: razorpay.com/privacy.

From Razorpay, our server reads the payment ID, order ID, plan, amount, payment status, payment time and the email address on the payment. It uses them only to confirm the purchase and create your pass, then discards them.

Our server

The game talks to our server (hosted on Vercel) only when you buy Premium, restore a purchase, or download premium packs. The server has no database and does not save your name, email, payment details or game data.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, time and the address requested) to keep the service secure and running. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Restoring a purchase

To restore Premium on another device you enter your payment ID and the email you paid with. These are sent to our server, checked against Razorpay and then forgotten. Nothing is stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your information, except with Razorpay to process payments and with our hosting provider to run the server.
  • The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

DareUp is meant for players aged [13] and over. Younger children should play with a parent or another adult, for example with the Family & Kids pack. The Couples 18+ pack is for adults only and asks players to confirm they are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All traffic between the game and our server uses HTTPS. Premium passes are signed so they cannot be changed. Because the pass is stored on your device, anyone with access to your device can use it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top.
