Browsers can't read .nwd files, so the page sends the model to Autodesk Platform Services. Autodesk converts it and returns every element's properties. The file goes only to Autodesk, into a temporary bucket they delete after 24 hours.

You need an APS app once: sign in at aps.autodesk.com/myapps, create an app (type "Traditional Web App" or "Server-to-Server") with Data Management and Model Derivative APIs, and paste its keys below. Depending on your APS plan, conversions may use Autodesk cloud credits.
